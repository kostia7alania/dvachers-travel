## Legal timing and refusal

Recheck the current legal text before reuse. [Permenkumham 11/2024, amended Article 97](https://peraturan.bpk.go.id/Download/344251/Permenkumham%20Nomor%2011%20Tahun%202024.pdf), printed page 31, permits the ITK extension application within the stated window before expiry. Under paragraph 3, an application filed and paid before the existing stay ends is not counted as overstay solely because processing lasts longer. This is not guaranteed approval or permission to ignore document or biometric deadlines. Do not promise a 23:59 cutoff or instant payment settlement.

[Permenkumham 22/2023, Article 137](https://peraturan.bpk.go.id/Download/330147/Permenkumham%20Nomor%2022%20Tahun%202023.pdf), printed pages 84-85, gives a departure period in specified refusal procedures. For unmet requirements, paragraph 2 measures up to seven calendar days from the relevant return stamp in the passport. It is not an automatic week after the original expiry and does not erase existing overstay. Other refusal grounds follow a different procedure; follow the actual decision and officer's instruction.

[The ministry's May 2025 announcement](https://kemenimipas.go.id/berita-utama/tekan-angka-pelanggaran-keimigrasian-wna-wajib-ke-kantor-imigrasi-untuk-perpanjangan-izin-tinggal) requires office photo/interview from 29 May 2025, including VOA. Publish "apply and pay remotely", not "extend without visiting immigration".

The responsible office's notification controls the visit timing. The observed Palembang notice gave a maximum of three working days from notification. Its [published ITK page](https://palembang.imigrasi.go.id/web/izin-tinggal-keimigrasian/1DKlHi0TLEx9ygcs85_IKaGc7DQ-AmuBx-Yjz6XPGx4) separately states processing time and a short correction window. Do not merge those clocks or describe an unbooked Monday as a guaranteed issuance date.

## Writing a useful Telegram update

Start from what happened and the reader's practical consequence. Keep prices, refund premiums, payment status and issuance status distinct. Mark historical fares and current search results with their dates; do not claim that one airline's prices or refund rules apply to every flight.

For classic message compatibility, keep the post within 4,096 characters after entity parsing, with short paragraphs, a few headings and links near important claims. [Telegram's text-message formatting](https://core.telegram.org/bots/api#formatting-options) supports emphasis and inline links; a newer rich-message mode has separate capabilities and limits. A draft is not permission to publish to a channel or update old messages.

Preserve the owner's evidenced conversational rhythm without manufacturing profanity or errors. Treat forwards and generated drafts separately from author originals. Keep the unconfirmed hotel requirement explicit. For the 31 October refund follow-up, report that Traveloka showed merchant-side completion four minutes after submission, while the bank statement and final airline cancellation were not independently checked. Do not turn this one observed processing time into a universal promise or call the funds credited without bank evidence. In the observed case, the office visit and PDF correction were on 26 October 2026; issuance was verified from the new e-ITK and refreshed portal on 30 October, with expiry on 24 November. Do not turn issuance into evidence of unobserved photo/interview steps or a general hotel-booking exemption.
