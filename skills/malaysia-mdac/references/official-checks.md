# MDAC source and field checklist

**Government source check: 2026-11-24, Asia/Kuala_Lumpur.** A verified source check is **not** a verified successful submission.

## Official references

- Official MDAC landing and registration: https://imigresen-online.imi.gov.my/mdac/main
- Official form (navigate via landing): https://imigresen-online.imi.gov.my/mdac/register
- Malaysian Immigration Department exemptions and MDAC announcements: https://www.imi.gov.my/index.php/en/pengumuman/malaysia-digital-arrival-card-mdac-for-foreign-visitors-2/
- Government service listing (MDAC is online; payment not applicable): https://rai.malaysia.gov.my/digital-services/apply---malaysia-digital-arrival-card-mdac
- Immigration Department warning of fake MDAC websites: https://www.imi.gov.my/index.php/pengumuman/makluman-portal-palsu-malaysia-digital-arrival-card-mdac/

## Published facts and changing details

- **Most foreign visitors** need MDAC; there are exceptions. Determine eligibility from the *latest official notices* rather than a frozen nationality list.
- Government registration form states arrival trip must be **within 3 days, including submission day**.
- Form uses dates formatted **DD/MM/YYYY** and includes **Drag To Verify**.
- Government service listing reports **no payment** applicable; MDAC and eVisa are distinct.
- Immigration Department warns about fraudulent lookalike MDAC portals.

## Actual form fields, as shown on 2026-11-24

| Part | Labels |
| --- | --- |
| Personal | Name; Passport No.; Date of Birth; Nationality / Citizenship; Place of Birth; Sex; Date of Passport Expiry |
| Contacts | Email Address; Confirm Email Address; Country / Region Code; Mobile No. |
| Trip | Date of Arrival; Date of Departure; Flight / Vessel / Transportation No.; Mode of Travel; Last Port of Embarkation before Malaysia |
| Stay | Accommodation of Stay; Address (In Malaysia); State; Postcode; City |
| Human check | Drag To Verify |

The current form asks for **alphanumeric-only** address input; follow live field validation. Do not invent missing fields. Do not publish applicants' data.

## Limits of testing

The site and field labels were checked from public government search content. A separate attempt to open the form via a connected remote Mac's automated browser returned a security block (“Web Page Blocked”, code 20000051). That is **not** proof that the traveler's own phone or the form itself cannot work. No full successful automated submission has been verified. If blocked, use the official site on the traveler's own device. Do not evade the block.
